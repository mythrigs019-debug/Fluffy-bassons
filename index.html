Academic Life

Crack the CSE Curriculum

Amrita's CSE program is challenging but incredibly rewarding. Here's what you need to know from day one.

📋

Academic Structure

4 years × 8 semesters. Each semester has theory subjects, labs, and assessments. Your CSE journey includes core CS, maths, and electives from Sem 3+.

Theory Lab Sessions Internals Sem Exams
🎯

Attendance Matters

Amrita requires 85% minimum attendance to appear for exams. One month's bunking can debar you. Mark your classes religiously — use the student portal to track.

  • Missing 1 class = -1.5% of monthly attendance
  • Labs count separately — never bunk labs
  • Medical leaves need doctor's notes within 3 days
📈

CGPA Improvement

Target 8.5+ CGPA to be competitive for placements and higher studies.

Average (6–7)60–70%
Good (8–8.5)80–85%
Excellent (9+)90%+
📖

Study Strategies

  • Use Pomodoro: 25 min study + 5 min break
  • Revise within 24 hours of every lecture
  • Form study groups for tough subjects
  • Solve previous year question papers
  • Use GeeksforGeeks for CS concepts
  • Start assignments early — never night-before
🧪

Lab Sessions

Labs are graded on viva + execution + record. Write your lab records neatly on time. Practice programs before lab day — never rely on copying.

  • C Programming Lab (Sem 1)
  • Data Structures Lab (Sem 3)
  • DBMS & OS Lab (Sem 4–5)
  • Mini Project Lab (Sem 6)
⚡

Internal Exams

Two internal exams per semester — your best score is typically counted. Internals contribute 40% to your final grade.

  • Cover first 3 units for Internal 1
  • Cover units 4–5 for Internal 2
  • Ask faculty for model papers
  • Attend doubt-clearing sessions

Fresher Survival Kit

The Unwritten Rules of College Life

The things seniors wish someone had told them on Day 1.

✅ Do's — Green Flags

  • Attend every lecture in the first two weeks — build the habit early
  • Introduce yourself to at least 5 new people each week
  • Create a LinkedIn profile in Week 1
  • Visit the library and explore resources on Day 3
  • Join at least one technical and one cultural club
  • Set up GitHub before your first lab session
  • Talk to seniors — they have invaluable shortcuts
  • Save your professor's email — you'll need it
  • Start a daily journal or Notion workspace for notes
  • Call home regularly — mental health matters

⛔ Don'ts — Red Flags

  • Never miss attendance without a valid medical reason
  • Don't copy lab records blindly — understand every line
  • Avoid staying up all night before exams — it backfires
  • Don't compare your pace with others — everyone is different
  • Never submit assignments on the last night carelessly
  • Don't ignore mental health warning signs — seek help early
  • Avoid excessive social media during study hours
  • Don't burn bridges with classmates — you need each other
  • Never skip meals to study — nutrition affects concentration
  • Don't wait until Sem 4 to learn coding — start now

⏱ Time Management Techniques

25
minutes of focused work

Pomodoro Technique

25 min work + 5 min break. After 4 cycles, take a 20-min break. Kills procrastination instantly.

2–4
priority matrix quadrants

Eisenhower Matrix

Split tasks by urgency × importance. Do, Schedule, Delegate, or Delete. Clears mental clutter.

1
weekly planning session

Sunday Planning

Every Sunday, plan your full week. Map deadlines, club times, and study blocks using Notion or Google Calendar.

3–5
deep work hours per day

Time Blocking

Block 3–5 hours of distraction-free deep work. Phone on silent. Use library or quiet rooms.

First Semester Success Roadmap

Your Month-by-Month Game Plan

A step-by-step blueprint to make Semester 1 count. Don't skip steps — each builds on the last.

01
Month 1 — July/Aug
Settle In & Connect
  • Introduce yourself to everyone
  • Explore the campus fully
  • Attend all club fairs & open events
  • Set up your digital workspace
  • Create LinkedIn & GitHub
02
Month 2 — Sep
Nail Academics
  • Build a study group
  • Prep for Internal Exam 1
  • Start lab record writing early
  • Install all essential tools
  • Read subject syllabi thoroughly
03
Month 3 — Oct
Learn GitHub
  • Complete Git basics course
  • Push your first repo
  • Contribute to a small project
  • Attend at least one hackathon
  • Join Technical Club officially
04
Month 4 — Nov
Start DSA
  • Begin Arrays on LeetCode (Easy)
  • Solve 3 problems per week
  • Study C++/Java syntax
  • Read GFG daily for 20 min
  • Internal 2 preparation
05
Month 5 — Dec
Join & Lead
  • Take on a club sub-role
  • Organise a small event
  • Build your first mini project
  • Update LinkedIn with skills
  • Semester end-exam revision
06
Month 6 — Jan
Build & Reflect
  • Push completed project to GitHub
  • Write a beginner blog post
  • Plan Semester 2 goals
  • Get result & analyse weak areas
  • Celebrate your growth! 🎉
